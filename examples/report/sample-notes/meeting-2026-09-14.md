# Ops review, 14 Sep 2026

Attendees: infra, support, finance.

Raw numbers from the dashboard, copied as-is:

- 1,412 active tenants, up from 1,190 in June.
- Median API latency 86 ms, p99 1.9 s. The p99 is the complaint we keep getting.
- Two incidents in Q3, both the same cause: the nightly export job holding a lock
  on the reporting replica.
- Support ticket volume 340/month, roughly 40% of them "export is slow or stuck".

Finance pushed back on the plan to buy a second replica (~€900/month). Their point:
the lock contention is a scheduling problem, not a capacity problem, and we should
prove we cannot schedule around it before spending.

Infra disagrees — says the export takes 4.5 h on the current hardware and the
window between the last EU write and the first US write is only about 3 h.

Nobody has measured what the export would take on the newer instance class. Action
item, unassigned.

Open question: do we actually need nightly exports, or would hourly incremental
ones be cheaper overall? Nobody owns the export requirement any more; it came from
a customer who churned in 2024.
